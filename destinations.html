Kribi & the Lobé Waterfalls

1,000–2,000 XAF

Chutes de la Lobé Waterfalls

Dramatic falls where a river tumbles straight into the Atlantic Ocean — one of the few places in the world this happens. Surrounded by rainforest, with boat and canoe rides available to get close.

📅 Dry season (Nov–Feb) for stronger flow ⏱ 2–4 hours

Boat rides negotiated separately, typically 5,000–10,000 XAF.

View on Google Maps
Free

Ngoye Beach

Kribi's central beach strip — golden sand, calm water, and a relaxed local scene with food stalls nearby.

📅 Early morning or late afternoon ⏱ 1–3 hours

Food and drinks from vendors are pay-as-you-go, at low cost.

View on Google Maps
Free

Grand Batanga

A quiet fishing village a bit south of town, good for escaping the touristy strip and seeing everyday coastal life.

📅 Mornings, when pirogues come in ⏱ 1–2 hours

Bring cash if you want to buy fish or a meal locally.

View on Google Maps
40,000–80,000 XAF/night

Hôtel-Restaurant Les Gîtes de Kribi

A well-regarded beachfront lodge along the road to Lobé Falls, known for its pool, jacuzzi, and sea-view restaurant — a good base for exploring rather than a sightseeing stop itself.

📅 Anytime, best in dry season ⏱ Overnight, or 1–2 hrs for lunch
View on Google Maps
5,000–12,000 XAF

Mykonos Paradise Kribi

A beachfront restaurant where you dine with your feet in the water as the sun sets over the Atlantic — string lights, fruit cocktails, and waves at your table.

📅 Sunset, around 6–6:30pm ⏱ 2–3 hours

Cocktails run roughly 3,000–5,000 XAF.

View on Google Maps
