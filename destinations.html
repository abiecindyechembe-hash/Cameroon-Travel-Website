Top Tourist Attractions in Cameroon

Here are five must-see destinations spanning Cameroon's coast, highlands, north and west.

Mount Cameroon

Best time to visit: November–February (dry season)

West Africa's highest peak (4,040m) and an active volcano near Buea, popular for multi-day treks and the annual "Race of Hope" mountain race.

View on Google Maps

Kribi & the Lobé Waterfalls

Best time to visit: November–February

A relaxed coastal town with golden beaches, fresh seafood, and the rare Lobé Waterfalls, where a river tumbles directly into the Atlantic Ocean.

View on Google Maps

Waza National Park

Best time to visit: November–April (dry season, best game viewing)

A UNESCO Biosphere Reserve in the far north, home to elephants, giraffes, lions and hundreds of bird species across open savannah.

View on Google Maps

Rhumsiki Peak

Best time to visit: October–February

A dramatic volcanic plug rising above the Mandara Mountains near the Nigerian border, famous for its jagged skyline and Kapsiki villages.
