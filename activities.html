Things to Do in Cameroon

From volcano treks to royal palaces, here's how to fill your Cameroon itinerary.

Adventure Sports

Trek Mount Cameroon over 2–4 days, go canyoning near Ekom-Nkam Falls, mountain bike through the Western Highlands, or surf along the Atlantic coastline near Limbe.

Local Food

Taste ndolé (bitterleaf stew with peanuts), achu with yellow soup, grilled poisson braisé by the coast, and poulet DG — a festive chicken and plantain dish loved nationwide.

Shopping

Browse the artisan workshops of Foumban for bronze work and wood carvings, hunt for fabrics and beadwork at Douala's Marché Central, or pick up Bamileke masks and sculptures in Bafoussam.

Sightseeing

Visit the Reunification Monument in Yaoundé, the Chutes de la Lobé where a river meets the sea, the Bamenda Ring Road highlands, and the historic German-era architecture of Buea and Douala.

Cultural Experiences

Tour the Sultan's Palace in Foumban, witness a Bamileke chieftaincy ceremony, or time your visit with the Ngondo Festival — a major water-spirit celebration held by the Sawa people of Douala each December.

Top 10 Things to Do

  1. Trek to the summit of Mount Cameroon
  2. Relax on Limbe's black-sand beaches
  3. Visit the Sultan's Palace and Museum in Foumban
  4. Go on a game drive in Waza National Park
  5. See the Lobé Waterfalls in Kribi
  6. Explore the rock formations of Rhumsiki in the Mandara Mountains
  7. Visit rescued primates at the Limbe Wildlife Centre
  8. Shop for bronze and wood crafts in Foumban's artisan quarter
  9. Attend a traditional Bamileke or Bamoun cultural ceremony
  10. Explore Yaoundé's Reunification Monument and Mvog-Betsi Zoo

Travel Tips

  • A visa is required for most visitors — apply in advance through the nearest Cameroonian embassy.
  • Carry small denominations of Central African CFA francs (XAF) for markets and rural areas.
  • French is dominant in most regions; English is widely spoken in the Northwest and Southwest.
  • Yellow fever vaccination is typically required for entry — check current health requirements.
  • Hire a licensed local guide for Mount Cameroon treks and northern national parks.
  • Respect local customs when visiting palaces and villages; ask permission before taking photos of people.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a visa to visit Cameroon?
Yes, most foreign visitors need a visa, obtainable from a Cameroonian embassy or consulate before arrival.
What is the best time of year to visit?
The dry season, roughly November to February, offers the best conditions for trekking, wildlife viewing, and beach visits.
What languages are spoken in Cameroon?
Cameroon is officially bilingual, with French and English as national languages, alongside more than 200 local languages.
